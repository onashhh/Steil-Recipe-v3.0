    Timers use absolute end timestamps + Screen Wake Lock so sleeping never pauses countdowns, plus a 📱 Phone Timer button launches your native Clock app.

    Scaling a batch updates quantities inside cooking steps automatically, and toggling Metric/Original never clears your checked ingredients or steps.

    Pressing Back exits Focus Mode without losing your active recipe progress, and AI Chef keeps a full conversation history inside Cooking Mode.
